# Icons

v3's hand-drawn icons were uneven: different optical sizes, corners and stroke ends. v4 uses **Lucide** (ISC licence, version 0.460.0): one 24 px grid, round caps and joins, and about 1,500 glyphs, so new needs are covered without drawing.

- **Stroke** is 1.6 px at every size (non-scaling), equal to the stem of 15 px Inter at 550. The Tune card sets it to 1.25, 1.6 or 2.
- **Size** equals the line height of the text beside it: 20 px next to titles, 16 px next to secondary lines, 24 px in app tiles.
- **No sparkle.** "Ask Tin" uses a question bubble; the assistant is not decorated as magic.
- **Third-party apps** use Android's themed monochrome icons, drawn in `ink` on the glass tile.
